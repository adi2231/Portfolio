Graduate case studies

Alvalade XXI payment system failure

Project Coordinator · Risk Management group project

Analyzed the real-world failure of Cartão 21, a pre-paid stadium payment system that broke down publicly during a stadium's inauguration. As project coordinator on a five-person team, built a full risk management plan using a Risk Breakdown Structure, SWOT analysis, and Monte Carlo simulation, then applied FMEA and a probability-impact matrix to prioritize findings. The analysis flagged final payment system deployment as the single highest-risk task in the schedule, the same task that caused the real system's public failure.

Skills: risk breakdown structure (RBS), SWOT analysis, Monte Carlo simulation, FMEA, probability-impact risk matrix, schedule risk analysis, stakeholder analysis
